# Trying the Pocket Kantei test version

Thank you for helping us.
Pocket Kantei is a chat app where you talk with Nico, a Four Pillars (BaZi) reader. Nico reads from your date of birth (and, if you know them, your birth time and place).
Before we launch, we would love to hear where it felt strange or hard to understand.

- Period: one week, [date] to [date]
- It is free (use it as much as you like)

## Getting started (1 minute)
1. Open the link we sent you on your phone.
   - If it arrived in a chat app, tap "…" → "Open in browser" (Safari or Chrome). The microphone may not work inside chat apps.
2. Follow the steps and enter your date of birth.
3. Then just talk to Nico.

## What we'd like you to try
Use it once or a few times a day, whenever you like. For example:
- "What kind of person am I?" / "How is this year for me?"
- "How is my money luck?" / "When will love move for me?" / "When should I change jobs?"
- Compatibility with someone (enter their date of birth)
- The "in detail" and "Explain the reasoning using my chart" buttons
- The microphone button to talk instead of typing

## If something feels off
Send a **screenshot** and **one line** to [contact]. For example:
- Nico says the same thing again and again
- Nico did not answer what you asked
- The reply is too long to read
- A sentence you could not understand
- It felt completely off
- You could not tell how to use it, or it stopped / showed an error

Good moments are welcome too.

## After one week
We will send a short survey (about 5 minutes).

## Please note
- We may read the conversations to improve the app. We will delete them after the test.
- Please do not write your full name, address, phone number, or anyone else's personal details.
- This test is for people aged 18 and over.
- Readings are for reference. If you are going through a hard time, please also reach out to a professional support line.
