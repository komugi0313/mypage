Privacy Policy

Effective: October 26, 2026

16TYPE+ ("the Site") is a free personality test run by 72k Inc. ("we"). This policy explains how we handle information on the Site.

1. Your birth date and answers

Your birth date, your answers and the type calculation all stay on your device (in your browser). We do not send them to a server or store them.

2. Information saved on your device

The following is saved only in your browser (local storage) and is never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data deletes all of it.

  • Your last birth date and result, and quiz progress
  • Results you saved with "Save this result" and the names you gave them
  • Your type record (dates, types, notes)
  • Names and types you entered for group compatibility
  • Recently shown questions (so you don't get the same ones)
  • Whether you closed the language notice

3. Analytics

We use Google Analytics (Google LLC) to improve the Site. It uses cookies to collect pages viewed, device and browser, and approximate region. The Site also sends:

  • Your result types (the four letters for temperament and character) and the page language
  • Taps on buttons such as share, save and compatibility

We do not send your nickname or birth date. See Google's page for how Google uses this data. You can block cookies in your browser settings.

4. Compatibility invite links

An invite link contains your types (temperament and character), your nickname if you entered one, and a character number (1–60) derived from your birth date. It does not contain your birth date itself. These are visible to the person who receives the link and to the app used to send it.

5. Third-party services

  • The Site is hosted on Google Firebase Hosting, which may keep access logs.
  • We use Google Fonts to display text.
  • Sharing and sending use services such as X, LINE and KakaoTalk, run by their own companies.

6. About the test

This test is not a medical or psychological assessment. Please enjoy the results for fun. The Site is not affiliated with MBTI® or 16Personalities.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when posted on this page.
