Beyond your 16 type

Is your 16 type
the one you were born with?

Your 16-type result shows your "character," shaped by experience. Your birth date shows your "temperament," the nature you were born with. Psychology also separates the two: temperament is what you are born with, character is what grows through experience. Neither ranks above the other. Together, they make you who you are. Setting them side by side shows the shape of your true self, and where you tend to struggle.

What makes these questions different

  1. No vague questionsInstead of "What kind of person are you?", we ask about real moments: "Did you do this lately?" You get the you of today, not the you that you wish to be.
  2. No "neither" optionYou answer on a 6-point scale, so you always lean a little one way. No more getting stuck in the middle.
  3. The same thing, asked from both sidesLike "Being with people energizes me" and "Time alone calms me," half the questions point the opposite way. This cancels out the habit of just saying "yes."
  4. We also measure how much you "waver"If your answers to opposite questions don't line up, that letter shows as "tends to waver" in your result. It explains why your 16 type keeps changing.
  5. Clear answers finish earlyYou first answer 6 questions per scale. Only where your answers land near the middle do we ask more to make sure. If you're clear-cut, it's 30 questions; the more you waver, the more carefully we ask (up to 60).
  6. Compared with your temperament's 5 lettersWe line up the 5 letters of your temperament from your birth date, and show where they match and where they differ.
