In this test, what you were born with is called temperament, and what grew through experience is called character. Temperament is our own reading, based on Four Pillars, an East Asian way of reading your birth date. Neither one is better. Putting the two side by side shows what makes you you, and where you tend to struggle.

What makes these questions different
  1. No vague questionsInstead of "What kind of person are you?", we ask about real moments: "Did you do this lately?" You get the you of today, not the you that you wish to be.
  2. No "neither" optionYou answer on a 6-point scale, so you always lean a little one way. You won't end up with no result because every answer was in the middle.
  3. The same thing, asked from both sidesLike "Being with people energizes me" and "Time alone calms me," half the questions point the opposite way. This makes it less likely you'll just say "yes" out of habit.
  4. We also measure how much you "waver"If your answers land near the middle, or your answers to opposite questions don't line up, that letter shows as "tends to waver" in your result. It explains why your 16 type keeps changing.
  5. Clear answers finish earlyThere are up to 60 questions. You start with 30. After that, we only ask about what was hard to call, until it becomes clear. Most people finish in about 30–50. The more you waver, the more carefully we ask, so your result is more accurate.
  6. Compared with your temperament's 4 lettersWe line up the 4 letters of your temperament from your birth date, and show where they match and where they differ. As a bonus, we also compare a 5th letter, your "mindset" (A: Assertive / T: Turbulent).
