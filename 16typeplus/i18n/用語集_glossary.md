It compares the user's **current type** (from a 60-question test or the 16-type result they already know) with their **born type** (derived from their birth date via Four Pillars astrology — but astrology jargon is NEVER shown on screen). Purpose: give hints that make work and relationships a little easier. Tone: calm, clinic-like, warm, plain, never insulting. Keep the brand "16TYPE+" untranslated.
