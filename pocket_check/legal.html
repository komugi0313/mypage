Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

72k Inc. ("we," "us") handles user information in "Pocket Reading" (the "Service") as follows.

1. Information We Handle and Where It Lives

(1) Stored only on your device

Your exact birth date, birth time, birthplace, conversation (chat) history, and settings such as language and theme are stored only on your device (browser/app local storage) and are not stored on our servers. Deleting the app or clearing browser data removes them.

(2) Stored on our servers (registration & attributes)

To improve and analyze the Service and to contact you, we store the following on infrastructure operated by our providers: email address, nickname, gender, age group, relationship status, topics / interests / usage frequency / occupation, referral source, country and language, and a randomly generated device identifier. Your exact birth date and the content of your conversations are not included in this storage. We collect and store this only after you consent during initial registration.

(3) Information about a partner entered for compatibility

When you enter a partner's birth date, gender, age group, and (optional) relationship for a compatibility reading, we store it to provide the reading and improve the Service. As this concerns a third party, we do not collect the partner's name. Please be considerate of that person before entering their data.

(4) Transmitted to generate readings

The messages you send and a summary of your chart computed on your device are transmitted, via our relay server, to an external AI service (a generative AI API provided by Google LLC) solely to generate responses. We do not use this data for advertising or sell it to third parties. Handling by the external AI service is subject to that provider's terms and policies.

(5) Collected to protect the Service

To enforce fair-use limits, a randomly generated device identifier and your IP address are stored on our server with daily usage counts for a short period. They are not used to identify individuals.

2. Purposes of Use

  • Generating and delivering readings
  • Managing usage limits per plan and preventing abuse
  • Analyzing registration data and attributes to improve the Service and understand trends
  • Contacting you and responding to inquiries
  • Improving the Service and fixing issues
  • Complying with applicable laws

3. Third Parties and International Transfer

We do not provide or sell your information to third parties except as required by law. As described above, information is transmitted to the external AI provider (Google LLC) to generate responses, and to infrastructure providers we use for hosting, form intake, and database (e.g., Netlify, Supabase). Their servers may be located outside Japan (e.g., in the United States), involving cross-border transfer of personal data.

4. Advertising and Tracking

The Service currently uses no advertising or behavioral tracking technologies (third-party cookies, ad SDKs, etc.). If introduced, we will update this policy and give prior notice.

5. Security

Communications are encrypted with TLS. Information stored on our servers is managed under appropriate access controls. Credentials such as API keys are managed server-side and are not included in user devices or client code.

6. Children

The Service is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Deleting Your Information

On-device information can be deleted via "Reset chat" in the app, deleting the app, or clearing browser data. Server-side usage counters are automatically purged after a period of time. You may request disclosure, correction, or deletion of the registration data, attributes, and partner information stored on our servers by contacting info@72k.ai.

8. Contact

For inquiries about this policy:
72k Inc. — Contact: info@72k.ai

9. Changes
