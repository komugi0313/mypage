Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026 · Operated by 72k Inc. (72k株式会社), Japan
The short version: in the app, your birth details stay on your device. When you ask for a reading, your chart facts and your question are sent to our language-processing provider to compose the text of that reading — nothing more. If you separately opt in to the free morning letter (email), you knowingly give us your email and birth details so we can build and send it — see §5. Payments are handled by the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play); we never see your card. We do not sell your personal data.

2. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or rent your personal information — to anyone.
  • We do not use your birth data or questions to train our own models or build advertising profiles.

3. Service providers

We use a small number of processors strictly to run the Service: Apple and Google (distribution and billing through their app stores), a hosting provider (delivery of the app), and a language-processing provider (composing reading text from your chart facts and question). Each receives only what it needs for its function.

4. Sensitive-data note

Birth details and the questions you ask can be deeply personal. That is why the default design keeps your birth data on your device, sends questions only transiently to compose your reading, and stores no reading history on our servers unless a future feature explicitly offers it — in which case this policy will be updated first and the choice will be yours.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Birth data on your device: delete the app (or clear its data) and it is gone.
  • Account email: kept while your account exists; deleted on request or upon account deletion.
  • Morning letter (email): every letter carries a one-tap unsubscribe link — using it stops all future email immediately. Every letter also carries a “manage your data” link where you can view and erase your optional profile (name, gender, role, country of origin) yourself, at any time, while continuing to receive the letter. The email and birth details needed to build the letter are kept only while you stay subscribed, and are removed on unsubscribe or on request.
  • You may request deletion of any data we hold at the contact below; we will respond within 30 days.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, California under the CCPA/CPRA, the EU/UK under the GDPR, or Japan under APPI), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To exercise them, contact us below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

Some optional details for the morning letter — such as gender identity or country of origin — may count as sensitive personal information. Giving them is entirely optional, we use them only to personalize your letter, and you can exercise your right to limit or delete them yourself at any time using the “manage your data” link in any letter (see §5).

7. Children

The Service is for adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

We are a Japanese company; our providers may process data in the United States, Japan and other countries, under contractual safeguards appropriate to those transfers.

9. Security

We use industry-standard measures (encryption in transit, least-privilege access). No method is 100% secure, but the less we hold, the less can go wrong — which is why we hold as little as possible.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a material way, we will announce it in the app before the change takes effect.
